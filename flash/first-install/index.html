EntropyLab — first installation

Only Waveshare ESP32-P4-WIFI6-Touch-LCD-4.3, PCB rev1.3, 32 MiB. Check the printed board revision yourself: USB cannot identify the PCB or authenticate your board. Unknown models are not supported.

Destructive factory replacement: factory demo, settings and overlapping provisioning/NVS/OTA/PHY data will be lost. This page does NOT back up or export your flash. Back up/export and read the recovery instructions first. Restoration is not guaranteed. Do not use with secrets or funded wallets.


Diagnostic loads a RAM stub, but writes no firmware. Each attempt is one-shot.

Read instructions and verify images before selecting a port.

After a diagnostic or any ended attempt, reload, verify and select again. After successful readback, press RESET manually. Failure is not success; do not retry blindly. Check boot, touch, public D6 test, Back, and retained Test results.
